PROJECT OBJECTIVES

1. To develop a web-based Personal Finance Manager application.

2. To help users track and manage their income and expenses effectively.

3. To provide budget management features for better financial control.

4. To generate financial reports for analyzing spending patterns.

5. To improve financial planning and decision-making.

6. To maintain financial records in a secure and organized manner.

7. To reduce the complexity of manual financial management.

8. To encourage better saving and spending habits among users.
